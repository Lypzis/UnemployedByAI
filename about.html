About

UnemployedByAI.lol is a satirical website that pretends to estimate whether a robot is learning how to do your job. It is built for laughs, screenshots, and mild professional discomfort.

The results are randomly generated jokes, not career advice, labor market analysis, or a secret message from your manager. Please do not quit, panic, or update LinkedIn because a cartoon robot said something rude.
