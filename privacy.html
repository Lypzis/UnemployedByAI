Privacy policy

Privacy

UnemployedByAI.lol is a static satire website. The profession you type is processed in your browser to generate a joke result. It is not sent to a server by this website.

The Interview Simulator also runs in your browser. Role requirements, pasted resume text, and interview answers are processed locally and are not sent to this website's server.

Information We Collect

We do not collect names, accounts, passwords, payment details, or user profiles. There is no login and no database for user-submitted professions, resumes, interview answers, or results.

Analytics And Ads

This website uses Google Analytics through the Google tag to understand page views, traffic sources, approximate location, browser and device information, and general site usage. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement. We do not intentionally send profession input, resume text, interview answers, names, emails, or payment details to Google Analytics.

This website may display ads served by Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Google and third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve ads, limit how often ads appear, measure ad performance, and help prevent fraud.

Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on a user's visits to this website and other websites. Users can manage or opt out of personalized ads through Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn about opting out of some third-party vendors' personalized advertising cookies at aboutads.info.

Where required by law, including for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, this website should request consent before using cookies or similar technologies for personalized advertising.
